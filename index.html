Computer or AI?

6-8.CS.b.1 · 6-8.CT.b.1 · rules vs. learning

The difference in one line

Computer program: a human writes the rules, the machine follows them exactly. "If temperature is below 68°, turn on heat."

AI: a human gives the machine thousands of examples, and it figures out its own rules. "Here are 50,000 photos of good and bad parts. Learn the difference."
